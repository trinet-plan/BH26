Case 3 — Patient ID: DEMO-CASE3-001 (fictional patient, Singaporean/Chinese ancestry)

History (fictional): A 31-year-old patient of Singaporean Chinese ancestry presented with exertional dyspnea and was found to have asymmetric septal hypertrophy on echocardiography (wall thickness 17 mm), with no secondary cause identified (normotensive, no history of athletic training).

Family history (fictional): The patient's father had a diagnosis of "thickened heart" in his 50s of unclear cause; no genetic testing was performed on him.

Genetic findings (real variant identities, fictional patient context): A cardiomyopathy gene panel identified two candidate variants:
1. MYH7 p.(Arg719Trp) — a well-established pathogenic variant (real ClinVar entry, see citation A)
2. MYBPC3 p.(Glu334Lys) — a variant previously classified pathogenic, but found at a carrier frequency of 13/4810 (0.03%) in the Singapore SG10K cohort; ClinVar currently lists this variant as having conflicting interpretations of pathogenicity (see citation B). The same variant has also been reported as a variant of uncertain significance in 3 patients in a Hong Kong Chinese HCM cohort (see citation C), reinforcing that its true population frequency in East Asian/Southeast Asian ancestry is higher than initially appreciated from predominantly European reference databases.

Clinical impression (fictional): Likely familial HCM; genetic result under specialist review given the presence of one well-established pathogenic variant (MYH7) alongside a second variant (MYBPC3) whose classification depends heavily on which population reference data is used.
